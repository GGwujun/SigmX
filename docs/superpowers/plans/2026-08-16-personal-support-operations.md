# Personal Support Operations Plan

**Goal:** Give administrators narrowly scoped, auditable support actions for individual users.

## Constraints

- No organizations, members, seats, service accounts, shared quotas, or enterprise CRM.
- Credit support is positive compensation only; it cannot silently confiscate purchased balance.
- Every mutation requires an operator reason and immutable audit entry.
- Device and Credential revocation verify the target belongs to the supplied individual user.
